General Conclusion
Delivery performance: Only 48.8% of orders are delivered on time. Delays are common in Semi-Urban 
areas and in Windy, Medium Traffic conditions.
Delivery vehicle: Van is fastest but more expensive. Motorcycle and Scooter balance cost and time for 
Metropolitan areas.
Effective shipper profile: Shipper aged 29-30, rating 4.6-4.7, operating mainly in Metropolitan, using 
Motorcycle.
Delivery cost by area & product: Semi-Urban has the highest cost. Grocery is the cost-optimized product 
group, whereas Jewelry and Home have high costs.
Delivery operation flow: The bottleneck occurs at the last mile of delivery: Pickup to On-time rate is only 
48.8% despite Pickup rate being almost 100%.
Overall operating cost: Average cost 8.70K VND/order, high cost ratio concentrated in Semi-Urban and 
Van areas.
Several Suggestions
Optimize operational resources: 
•Allocate Motorcycle/Scooter to Metropolitan to reduce costs.
•Review Van for Semi-Urban to balance speed and costs.
Improve shipper support policies:
•Apply bonus or financial support policy when delivering in Windy or Traffic Medium+ conditions.
•Train to handle traffic and weather incidents.
Optimize costs by product group:
•Prioritize promoting Grocery services.
•Review Jewelry and Home shipping policies to control costs.
Adjust operational processes:
•Develop optimal delivery routes for Semi-Urban.
•Increase on-time delivery rate by optimizing pickup and final delivery processes.
